Orders & Shipping

Where's my order?➜

Once a provider approves your intake, the pharmacy typically ships same day, and most orders arrive within 2–5 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email the moment it ships — check your dashboard if you can't find it.

Can I change my shipping address after ordering?➜

If your order hasn't shipped yet, update it from your dashboard or message us right away and we'll try to catch it before it leaves the pharmacy.

Billing & Plans

How do I pause or cancel my plan?➜

Head to your dashboard and select "Manage plan." Pausing and cancelling both take effect immediately, with no retention calls or hidden fees. If you're mid-protocol, your provider will flag anything worth knowing before you stop.

Do you offer refunds?➜

If you're not prescribed after your intake, you're never charged for medication. For everything else, see our Refunds & Cancellations policy for what qualifies and how to request one.

Treatment Questions

Can I switch medications or adjust my dose?➜

Yes — message your provider through the patient portal. Dosage and medication changes are a clinical decision, so they'll review your history before making an adjustment.

What if I have side effects?➜

Message your provider through the patient portal right away — most side effects are manageable with a dosage tweak, but they'll want to know regardless. For anything urgent, contact emergency services first.

Account & Privacy

I can't log in to my account.➜

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen to reset it. Still stuck? Email us and we'll get you back in.

Is my information kept private?➜

Yes. Vitakey is HIPAA-secure end to end — your health data is encrypted, stored securely, and never sold or shared for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
